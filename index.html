About

Apps that feel good to use and are ready for the Play Store

I'm Dilawar Zaman, a freelance Android developer from Pakistan who builds native apps and games with Java and Kotlin in Android Studio. My work focuses on offline-first apps: they load fast, respect users' privacy and keep working without a connection.

I handle the whole process: design and development, testing on real devices, signed AAB builds, store listings and Google Play Console publishing. I've built and shipped puzzle, arcade, board and runner games as well as everyday utilities for clients, all live on Google Play today.

Alongside Android, I work as a frontend and Shopify developer at Fixpertify, which keeps my HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Liquid skills sharp.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What kind of apps and games do you build?

Native Android apps and games: puzzle, arcade, runner, board and casual games, plus everyday utilities like photo resizers and document scanners. You can see live examples in the Work section above.

Do you use Java or Kotlin?

Both. I build native apps in Java and Kotlin with Android Studio, and use Jetpack Compose or Flutter when they suit the project better.

Can you publish my app on Google Play?

Yes. I prepare the signed AAB release build, write the store listing, and publish it through Google Play Console on your own developer account, so the app stays fully yours.

Can you fix or update an app I already have?

Yes. I fix crashes, ANRs, layout issues and Play Console warnings, and upgrade older projects to the latest target API, Gradle version and libraries so they meet current Google Play requirements.

Will I get the source code?

Yes. When the project is finished you receive the full source code along with the signing keystore and its passwords, so you can update the app yourself or with any developer later.

Do your apps work offline and respect privacy?

Wherever the app allows it, I build offline-first: fast to load, working without a connection, and asking only for the permissions it really needs.

How much does an app cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the features and design you need. Send me your idea and I'll reply with a clear plan, timeline and price before any work starts.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work with clients worldwide through Fiverr, email, LinkedIn and WhatsApp, and every app is tested on real devices before delivery.
